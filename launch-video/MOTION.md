## Review method

Inspect native captures first, then final encoded frames at every story beat and around camera ramps, keyboard cues and pickup cuts. Confirm the source and captions remain readable, native controls stay inside frame, themes settle, and short dictated text starts at the top. Inspect adjacent frames during tiling, floating and camera motion; stills alone do not establish smooth animation. Check compositor receipts for `floating=false/pinned=false` when tiled and `floating=true/pinned=true` after refloating and on desktop 2. The [Dwindle layout documentation](https://wiki.hypr.land/Configuring/Layouts/Dwindle-Layout/) describes the native split behavior; the [window-rule documentation](https://wiki.hypr.land/Configuring/Basics/Window-Rules/) distinguishes initial float/pin rules from subsequent user actions.

Check duration, frame cadence, decoding errors, black frames and encoded audio loudness independently. Transcribe the final mix to catch clipped or missing phrases. Those checks establish technical integrity and script coverage; natural delivery and musical taste still benefit from a human sound-on pass.
